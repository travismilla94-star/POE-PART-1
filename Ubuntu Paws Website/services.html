Our services

From rescue to rehoming, here is how we care for every animal that comes through our doors.

Rescue and rehabilitation

Every animal we take in is assessed by our consulting veterinarian, vaccinated, sterilised and, where needed, treated for injury or illness before being placed into our shelter or foster care network to recover and socialise.

How adoption works

  1. Submit an enquiry through our enquiry form.
  2. Meet the animal at our shelter or a foster carer's home.
  3. Complete a short adoption application and home check.
  4. Take your new companion home, with a follow-up call two weeks later.
